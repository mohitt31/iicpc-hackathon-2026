The pipeline, end to end.

Every order on this page travels this path. Contestant code never touches the host: it runs CPU-pinned inside an isolated sandbox while the fleet bombards it and the ingester measures from outside the box.

Submission & sandboxingISOLATION

Uploads are containerized and pinned: dedicated cores, hard memory caps, no outbound network. Malicious or runaway code burns its own budget, never a neighbour's.

Distributed load generatorSPEED

A CPU-pinned bot fleet — 8× oversubscribed per box and distributed across 3 real nodes (1.69 M orders, Sent==Acked) streams limit, market and cancel orders over binary TCP at the intended rate — it never slows down to flatter the engine.

Telemetry & validationACCURACY

Acks are TSC-stamped outside the sandbox. Price-time priority and fill accuracy are verified by a byte-exact diff against an offline replay — 25,743,624 bytes, zero mismatches.

Scoring & leaderboardSTABILITY

Per-bot HDR histograms merge additively — never averaged. Unstable runs fail the integrity gate and are excluded, not penalized. Deltas stream to the board over WebSocket.
